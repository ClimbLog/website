Beta

Fly with the beta

ClimbLog is still in development and testing on both platforms. Grab a build, log a few flights, and tell us what breaks.

The beta is the full app: flight logging, the map, friends, and the Passport. It updates often, sometimes several times a week, and the occasional rough edge is the point. Everything you log carries over to the released version.

Invites opening sooniPhone beta Invites opening soonAndroid beta

On iPhone

  1. Install Apple's TestFlight app from the App Store.
  2. Tap the iPhone beta button above on your iPhone and accept the invite.
  3. Install ClimbLog from inside TestFlight. Updates arrive there too.

On Android

  1. Tap the Android beta button above on your Android phone.
  2. Sign in with the Google account you want the build tied to, then accept the invite.
  3. Install the build. New versions show up as a notification.

Found something?

Bug reports are the whole reason the beta exists. Tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.
