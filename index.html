Features

Built for people who love flying, not filing paperwork

ClimbLog helps you keep your entire flight history in one place, from your very first trip to the routes you are taking next month.

Your Personal Flight Log

Save every flight with airports, airline, aircraft, date, seat details, and notes. Your travel history stays organized and easy to revisit.

Route Map and Globe View

See your routes come alive on a world map, then zoom out to a globe view that makes long-haul flights feel as dramatic as they should.

Stats, Milestones, and Trends

Track flights, miles, countries, airports, aircraft, longest routes, best year, and more without doing any manual math.

Upcoming Flights

Keep future trips separate from your history, so you can watch what is coming up next and open route details before you even take off.

Flight Passport Sharing

Create a polished share card from your travel data, complete with your route map, countries visited, and signature passport-inspired styling.

Friends and Shared Routes

Follow friends, compare where you have both flown, and browse upcoming trips in a feed that makes the social side of flying actually fun.
