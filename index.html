Features

Every flight you have taken, and everyone you fly with

Add a flight by searching a flight number, or enter it by hand. ClimbLog fills in the airports, aircraft, and times, then keeps the whole history organized for you.

Your personal flight log

Search a flight number and ClimbLog fills in the rest: airports, airline, aircraft, times. Add seat and notes yourself.

Route map and globe

Every route you have flown, drawn on a world map that spins out to a globe. Standard, satellite, and dark styles.

Stats and milestones

Miles, countries, airports, aircraft types, home airport, longest route, best year. Counted for you as you log.

Upcoming flights

Future trips stay separate from your history, then pick up gates, terminals, and delay status as departure gets close.

Flight Passport

A share card built from your real history, with your route map, countries, and passport-style detailing. Light or dark.

Friends and shared routes

Follow friends, see where you overlap, and watch who is heading somewhere next. The part other flight apps skip.

Friends

Flying is better when someone knows you landed

Most flight apps are built for one person tracking one trip. ClimbLog adds the part they leave out: the people you fly with, fly to see, and compare notes with afterwards.

  • A feed of what is coming up. See who is flying where next instead of finding out afterwards.
  • Shared routes. Compare maps with a friend and find the airports and routes you have both flown.
  • Follow a friend's flight. Tap the bell and get told when they take off, divert, or land. An Altitude feature.

Altitude

Go further with Altitude

ClimbLog is free and stays useful without paying. Altitude is for the people who fly enough to want the extras.

  • Friend flight alerts. Follow a friend's flight and get notified when it departs, diverts, or lands.
  • Historical flight lookup. Pull in flights from years back, not just the recent window.
  • Satellite and globe maps. Higher-fidelity map styles for the views worth showing off.
  • Alternate app icons and the gold ring. Small things, but you will know.
