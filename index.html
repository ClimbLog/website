Features

Built for people who love flying, not filing paperwork

Your whole flight history in one place, from the first trip you can remember to the route you are taking next month.

Your personal flight log

Save every flight with airports, airline, aircraft, date, seat, and notes. Search it back years later in seconds.

Route map and globe

Every route you have flown, drawn on a world map you can spin out to a globe. Long-hauls finally look the part.

Stats, milestones, and trends

Flights, miles, countries, airports, aircraft types, longest route, best year. No spreadsheet formulas required.

Upcoming flights

Future trips stay separate from your history, with gates, terminals, and delay status as departure gets close.

Flight Passport

A share card built from your real travel data, complete with route map, countries, and passport-style detailing.

Friends and shared routes

Follow friends, compare where you have both flown, and see who is heading somewhere next in a live feed.

Flight Passport

A travel identity worth sharing

Every ClimbLog account builds a Flight Passport from your real history. Countries, miles, home airport, and the shape of your route map, laid out like a travel document. Post it, send it, or keep it to yourself.

Altitude

Go further with Altitude

ClimbLog is free and stays useful without paying. Altitude is for the people who fly enough to want the extras.

  • Friend flight alerts. Follow a friend's flight and get notified when it departs, diverts, or lands.
  • Historical flight lookup. Pull in flights from years back, not just the recent window.
  • Satellite and globe maps. Higher-fidelity map styles for the views worth showing off.
  • Alternate app icons and the gold ring. Small things, but you will know.
