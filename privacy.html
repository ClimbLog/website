This Privacy Notice for KeyQueue LLC (doing business as ClimbLog) ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Download and use our mobile application (ClimbLog)
  • Use ClimbLog — a personal flight logging and tracking app
  • Visit climblog.co or contact us for support

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.

Summary of Key Points

  • What personal information do we process? Your account details, the flights you log, your profile and friends, and the settings you choose. See Section 1.
  • Do we process sensitive personal information? No.
  • Do we sell your information or use it for advertising? No. ClimbLog has no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Do we collect information from third parties? Only what's needed to run the app: your name and email from Apple or Google if you sign in with them, flight schedules and status from FlightAware, and subscription status from Apple, Google and RevenueCat.
  • Who else handles your information? A short list of service providers that run parts of the app for us, named in Section 4.
  • How do we keep your information safe? We use organizational and technical safeguards, though no transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
  • How do you delete your data? Delete your account in the app, or follow the steps at climblog.co/delete-account.html.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you give us

In Short: We collect the information you enter when you create an account and use ClimbLog.
  • Account details: your name, email address, username, and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Profile: your display name, avatar, home airport, and preferences such as units and appearance.
  • Flights: the flights you log or import (flight number, airline, airports, dates and times, seat, aircraft, notes, and similar details).
  • Travel Vault: the documents you add, which are only the document type, issuing country, and an optional expiry date. We do not ask for or store document numbers or images.
  • Friends and sharing: your friends list, friend requests, and your sharing settings.
  • Feedback and support: messages you send us in the app or by email.

Sensitive Information

We do not process sensitive personal information.

Payment Data

We never see or store your card details. Altitude subscriptions are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat manages subscription status for us. We receive your purchase history: which plan you have, when it started and renews, and whether it is active.

Sign in with Apple or Google

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name, your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead), and an account identifier, as described in Section 6.

Application Data

If you use the ClimbLog app, we may also collect or use the following:

  • Calendar (optional). If you turn on calendar import, the app reads events from the calendars you choose, on your device, to find flights. Calendar contents stay on your device; only the flights it finds (flight number, date, and airports) are saved to your account. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Push notifications (optional). If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can send flight alerts and friend updates. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your device's settings.
  • Reminders on your device. Travel Vault expiry reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Biometric Lock (optional). Face ID and fingerprint checks are handled entirely by your device. We never receive biometric information.
  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, and app version, used for crash reports and compatibility.
  • Travel Assistant conversations. If you use the Travel Assistant, your current conversation is saved on your device so it is still there when you return. If you subscribe to Altitude, your conversations are also stored in your account so they sync across your devices. You can delete individual conversations or clear all of them in the app at any time.

Information collected automatically

In Short: Crash reports and diagnostics help us keep the app working.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics: when the app crashes, freezes, or hits an error, it sends a report through Sentry. Reports include technical details (device model, operating system, app version, what the app was doing) and your account ID and username, but not your email address. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Bug reports: when you send a bug report from the app, it goes to Sentry with your message, display name, and email address so we can reply. Bug reports are kept for up to 90 days, and we delete yours sooner if you ask.
  • Server logs: our servers record technical information such as IP addresses and request times to operate the Services, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • Location: we do not collect your device's location. Your home airport and flights are information you enter yourself.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information from other sources

In Short: We receive limited information from the services that make ClimbLog work.
  • Apple and Google, if you sign in with them (see above).
  • FlightAware, which provides flight schedules, status, and tracks when you look up or track a flight.
  • Apple, Google, and RevenueCat, which tell us whether your subscription is active.

We do not buy personal information, and we do not receive information from data brokers or marketing partners.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

In Short: We use your information to provide ClimbLog, keep it secure, support you, and comply with law.
  • To provide the app. Create and secure your account, save your flights, build your stats, maps, and Flight Passport, and sync across your devices.
  • Flight lookups and alerts. Look up flight details and send the alerts you turn on.
  • Friends and sharing. Show your profile and the flights you choose to share to your friends.
  • Subscriptions. Unlock Altitude features and manage your subscription.
  • Safety and security. Prevent abuse and fraud, help confirm that requests come from the genuine app, limit request rates, and check usernames for offensive content.
  • Reliability. Diagnose and fix crashes and errors.
  • Support and feedback. Answer your messages and act on your feedback.
  • Legal obligations. Comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests.

3. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On to Process Your Information?

In Short: We only process your personal information when we have a valid legal reason to do so under applicable law.

If you are located in the EU or UK

  • Performance of a contract. To provide the Services you signed up for, including your account, flights, sharing, and any subscription.
  • Consent. For optional features such as calendar import and notifications. You can withdraw your consent at any time by turning the feature off.
  • Legitimate interests. To keep the Services secure, prevent abuse, and diagnose crashes.
  • Legal obligations. Where processing is necessary to comply with the law.
  • Vital interests. Where processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of any person.

If you are located in Canada

We may process your information if you have given us express or implied consent, or in exceptional circumstances as permitted by applicable law (such as fraud detection, business transactions, or compliance with legal requirements).

4. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

In Short: We share information with service providers that run parts of ClimbLog for us, with the other users you choose, and where the law requires. We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Service providers

These companies process information on our behalf, only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: accounts and sign-in, database, servers, app verification, and push notification delivery on Android.
  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple and Google, in-app purchases, and push notification delivery.
  • Expo: delivers push notifications to your device.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions and purchase history.
  • Sentry: crash reports, diagnostics, and bug reports you send from the app.
  • FlightAware: flight data. We send the flight number and date you look up or track.
  • OpenAI: checks usernames for offensive content. We send only that text.
  • Mapbox: maps in the Android app. Mapbox receives map requests from your device; its usage telemetry is turned off.

Other ClimbLog users

  • Your profile (display name, username, avatar, home airport, Altitude badge, flight stats summary, and join date) can be seen by other signed-in ClimbLog users, for example when they search for your username or open a link you share.
  • Your flight map and upcoming flights are shown to your friends according to your sharing settings, which you can change at any time.

Other situations

  • Legal requirements. When we believe disclosure is required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ClimbLog, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or part of our business.

5. Do We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies?

In Short: No advertising or analytics trackers.

The ClimbLog app does not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The climblog.co website does not use cookies; it only stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser.

6. How Do We Handle Sign in with Apple and Google?

In Short: If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive basic account information from them.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name, your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead), and an account identifier. We use this only to create and secure your ClimbLog account. If you delete your account, we also ask Apple to revoke ClimbLog's access to your Apple ID.

7. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

In Short: Until you delete your account, unless the law requires otherwise.

We keep your account information, flights, and settings for as long as you have a ClimbLog account. When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, flights, friends, Travel Vault entries, synced Travel Assistant conversations, and push tokens, ask RevenueCat to delete its record of you, and, if you signed in with Apple, ask Apple to revoke ClimbLog's access. Crash reports, bug reports, and server logs are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically. We may keep limited information where the law requires it, or to prevent fraud and abuse.

Travel Assistant conversations synced for Altitude subscribers are deleted automatically 12 months after they were last updated, when you delete them in the app, or when you delete your account.

Purchase records held by Apple or Google are governed by their own privacy policies.

8. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

In Short: We aim to protect your personal information through a system of organizational and technical security measures.

Information is encrypted in transit, and your sign-in session is stored in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). However, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

9. Do We Collect Information from Minors?

In Short: ClimbLog is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

The Services are not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. By using the Services, you represent that you are at least 13 years old and, if you are under the age of majority where you live, that a parent or guardian has agreed to your use of the Services. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete the account and that information promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us at support@climblog.co.

10. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

In Short: Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

In some regions (like the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and Canada), you have rights under applicable data protection laws, including the right to:

  • Request access and obtain a copy of your personal information
  • Request rectification or erasure
  • Restrict the processing of your personal information
  • Data portability (if applicable)
  • Not be subject to solely automated decision-making
  • Object to the processing of your personal information

If you are located in the EEA or UK and believe we are unlawfully processing your personal information, you have the right to complain to your Member State data protection authority or the UK data protection authority. If you are in Switzerland, you may contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

Withdrawing your consent

If we are relying on your consent to process your personal information, you can withdraw it at any time by turning off the feature in the app or by contacting us using the details in Section 14. This will not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Account Information

You can review and change your account information in the app at any time. To delete your account and its data, use Account › Delete Account in the app, or follow the steps at climblog.co/delete-account.html, or email support@climblog.co.

11. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature. Because ClimbLog does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, there is nothing for a DNT signal to turn off, and we do not change how we handle data in response to one.
